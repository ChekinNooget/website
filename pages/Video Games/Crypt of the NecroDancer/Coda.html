I play Coda a lot, and have beaten her dozens of times. Sometimes I get bored and do silly challenges.

Coda but with kazoos in the background

This is SilvaGunner's kazoo cover of the soundtrack. It makes it kind of hard to focus because the music is so offbeat, but it's managable.

Coda but with 1000% screenshake

This was a very silly challenge. I normally play with 0% screenshake because even the default amount of screenshake is distracting on double tempo. I had to think ahead a lot during this run because you can't really see what's going on while you're digging or attacking.

Peaceda

Essentially Dove + Coda. It's kind of awkward to play because confused enemies drop gold. Honestly, I lucked out with this run with blast helm, bomb charm, and plenty of bombs.

Coda but with permanent tempo up

I've been playing a lot of double tempo, so a little increase isn't too bad, comparatively speaking. 4-3 and Death Metal are the most problematic. Death Metal especially is what's keeping me back from attempting 2 tempo up traps.

Coda but with reversed zone order

This is hardly more difficult than a regular run, but it's worth including anyway. This is especially the case because there isn't any enemy zone bleed like with Aria's level generation. In any case, it is more difficult in that Zone 5 and Zone 4 are much harder without items, but it gets balanced out by the fact that if I die, it's not a huge annoyance, because it's so close to the beginning. Once I got past Zone 3, Zone 2 and Zone 1 were a breeze.

Coda but with permanent tempo up

This is a really weird one. By disabling the HUD, I had to remember everything that I picked up. This is especially dangerous if I miscount how many bombs I have left, if I miscount my damage, if I use a spell while it's still on cooldown, and so on and so forth. I dealt with these probelms by just not using them often, lol. Pulse did come in handy on the final boss though!

Coda but with Miku's soundtrack

This is very similar to a permanent tempo up trap, except with more variation in how fast the song is. On average, the soundtrack for the Hatsune Miku DLC is faster than the base game. Another caveat is that Shrine of Rhythm is riskier to take, because it's harder to count to 7 with some of the songs. Maybe someday I'll beat Coda with the Shovel Knight soundtrack, but that's gonna be in a while... 400BPM on two separate levels is no joke.

Coda but the tempo randomly challenges

This was probably my favorite challenge so far! It's really funny. I don't know exactly how much the tempo can vary, but it's at least one tempo up trap in each direction (faster or slower). Thankfully, NecroDancer's beat detection is very lenient, so I had plenty of time to react to the changes in tempo. It did catch me off guard a few times in previous runs, causing me to die, but I was able to hold it together for a complete All Zones run.

That's pretty much it for challenge runs! A few challenges I have on my bucket list are low%, war + boss shrine, Aria level gen, Tempo level gen, invisible gold, and some random presets with a bunch of mods cobbled together. I'm down to take any suggestions, so feel free to let me know if there's anything you'd want to see!
